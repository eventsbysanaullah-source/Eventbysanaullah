Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding booking, package customizations, and event execution.

What services does Events by Sanaullah provide?
We provide complete event management, venue booking assistance, stage design, fresh and artificial floral decorations, ambient lighting, audio-visual sound setups, catering, photography, and event coordination across weddings, birthdays, night parties, university functions, and corporate events.
Do you offer complete wedding event management?
Yes, we manage all wedding functions including Mehndi, Baraat, and Walima setups. Our team takes care of stage backdrops, floral pathways, seating layout, sound systems, catering setups, and on-site staff.
Can I customize decor themes and packages?
All our services are customizable. You can choose specific color themes, floral choices, lighting setups, and guest sizes, and we will build a package tailored to your budget.
How far in advance should I book my event?
For large functions like weddings and university galas, we recommend booking 3 to 6 weeks in advance. For birthday parties or smaller social functions, 1 to 2 weeks' notice is typically sufficient.
